# Negative-arm labeling sheet — duration probe

**One row = one recording = one label.** Each recording gives you three 20s excerpts, taken 15% / 50% / 85% of the way through it. Listen to all three, then write a single label in that row.

Judge **the whole recording**, not the excerpt you happen to like best: it only counts as a negative if it stays negative at every listening length.

Labels:

- `no-sahitya` — no sung lyrics anywhere: alapana, tanam, thani, tuning, applause, instrumental-only improvisation.
- `ooc` — a real composition with sung lyrics, but one you judge is NOT in our catalog.
- `in-catalog` — a composition with sung lyrics that IS in the catalog. Not a negative; it drops out of this arm.
- `unusable` — too noisy/broken to judge.

The filenames deliberately tell you nothing — the metric contract says strata come from the audio, not from metadata.
